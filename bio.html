Sasha Ricci Rovatti works in moving image, graphic design and text. Her practice often takes as a focus the ways histories are narrativised and shaped by visual politics.

Graduated with a bachelor's degree in Graphic Design and Visual Communication from ISIA Urbino in 2023, she worked in Bologna and Foligno in design for publishing. She then pursued a master's degree in the Critical Inquiry Lab department of the Design Academy Eindhoven, where she graduated with an award nominee project on past militant Italian cinema.
